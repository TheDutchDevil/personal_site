I am Nathan, an experienced software engineering researcher with a PhD and hands-on experience as a DevOps engineer. I enjoy working with people on complex problems that do not have an obvious solution yet: I analyze the problem, work out an approach, and turn the results into advice that others can act on. The breadth and quality of my research have been recognized with several national and international awards. From 2024 to 2026 I worked as a postdoctoral fellow at the University of Victoria in Canada, and I am currently based in the south of the Netherlands.

What I bring
========

* **Research.** I set up and carry out research projects using both qualitative and quantitative methods: experiments with developers, statistical analyses of large datasets, focus groups, systematic literature reviews, and replication studies. I put the findings into a broader context so that others can use them. This has led to 18 peer-reviewed [publications](publications), including in leading journals and conferences such as TOSEM, EMSE, and ICSE, and to more than 20 presentations at universities and conferences. I speak Dutch and English.
* **Research quality.** My PhD received the MSR Distinguished Doctoral Research Award for "excelling in the diversity of research methods and extraordinary methodological rigor". I also study how research itself can be improved, for instance in an [analysis](https://cassee.dev/files/multiverses.pdf) of how the analytical choices researchers make affect their results. I review for leading journals (TOSEM, TSE, and EMSE), and was a member of the awards committee of MSR 2026.
* **Supervision and teaching.** I have supervised more than 15 BSc, MSc, and PhD students, helping them choose and apply the right research methods so that they can carry out high-quality research independently. Several of these projects led to internationally published papers. I hold a University Teaching Qualification, and redesigned and taught the master's course on empirical research methods at TU/e together with Alexander Serebrenik, which was recognized for its excellent student evaluations. I also mentored interns in industry.
* **Leading research projects.** At the University of Victoria I led a multidisciplinary research project on how AI affects software development, in a team of six. I was responsible for the quality of the research, and I secured funding with my own proposal in the university's competitive Aspiration program.
* **AI and data analysis.** I have trained and evaluated AI models, including transformers, developed statistical models, and written algorithms in Python to efficiently process a 300 GB dataset, leading to published peer-reviewed articles to help improve the quality of quantitative software engineering research.
* **Software engineering and DevOps.** At Spendlab Technology I helped explore and prepare the migration to Azure of a C#/ASP.NET application that analyzes a large amount of payment data: I analyzed the existing application, helped update the architecture, and built a proof of concept for a version that can be hosted in Azure. I also set up fully automated releases, and helped design prototypes that the R&D department integrated into production systems.

Research
========

My research is about the human side of software engineering: how developers experience their work, and how teams can benefit from new tools such as AI without losing the skills, judgment, and collaboration that good software depends on. For my PhD I studied emotions and sentiment in software engineering; the full thesis is available [here](https://cassee.dev/files/PhD_Thesis_Nathan_Cassee.pdf). At the University of Victoria I studied AI, and found that relying on AI can lead to emotional disengagement that hinders skill development in novice developers, and that AI is disrupting social practices such as code review. I have also worked on technical debt, bots, continuous integration, and error handling by Apple Swift developers.

My work has been recognized with the MSR Distinguished Doctoral Research Award, a nomination for the 2025 Eindhoven University of Technology Best PhD Thesis, First Runner-up for the 2025 Informatics Europe Best Dissertation Award, and Third place at the VERSEN PhD Thesis Award. My master thesis was awarded first place at the VERSEN master thesis awards. See [recognition](recognition) for a complete overview.
